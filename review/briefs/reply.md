# Reply brief

Turn the round's ledger into replies to the open threads. Draft only; posting is the
owner's call.

- One reply per thread that a commit answers: what changed, in one sentence; the
  commit; the test name; the probe's red and green lines from the ledger, verbatim.
- A declined finding: the failure-mode sentence and its evidence.
- Never longer than the comment answered. No thanks, no praise, no "good catch", no
  restating the comment.
- A thread with nothing new gets no reply.
- Output: markdown, one section per thread headed by its URL.
