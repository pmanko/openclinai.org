Patient-Mediated HIE · FHIR R4 & IPS

WA Health Summary (WAHS+)

Patient-mediated longitudinal health summary and grounded clinical chart exploration across fragmented care sites.

Designed for Washington State's interoperability landscape, WAHS+ ingests multi-clinic FHIR R4 and International Patient Summary (IPS) bundles, enforces 42 CFR Part 2 and WA RCW 70.02 privacy segmentation, condenses multi-decade records across 7 token budgets (256k–1k), and runs pre-generation clinical entity graph and guideline checks.

Computable Guidelines · FHIR R4 & CQL

WHO SMART Guidelines L2→L3 Synthesis & Verification

Translating narrative clinical decision-support tables into executable FHIR Clinical Quality Language (CQL) verified against deterministic test suites.

Explores how open-weight clinical models and RL with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) can assist health-system implementers in authoring and auditing computable WHO SMART Guidelines (Immunizations and Antenatal Care), using cqf-ruler CQL execution against official L3 MeasureReport bundles as a zero-variance oracle.
