# WA Health Summary (WAHS) Public Pages

- `https://openclinai.org/wahs/` (`landing/wahs/index.html`): Project overview, embedded live Web App (`shl-ips`) and Mobile Companion demo videos (hosted on `gs://openclinai-media/wahs/`), 5-stage pipeline summary, and November 26 blinded H200 benchmark headlines.
- `https://openclinai.org/wahs/architecture/` (`landing/wahs/architecture/index.html`): Layered architecture guide and builder reference covering the 5-stage pipeline (`FHIR Sources -> Zero-Privilege Auth -> Extract & Condense -> Profile/Prompt & Enrichers -> LLM & Provenance`), Margaret Chen worked trace, isolation/caching layers, OpenAI `/v1` + SSE wire contract, declarative `profiles.yaml`, 5 generalizable enrichers, 64k warm `num_ctx` rules, and runtime env config.
- `https://openclinai.org/wahs/llm-strategy/` (`landing/wahs/llm-strategy/index.html`): Plain-language team strategy and design rationale covering what we tried, kept, and retired, November 26 Run 3A & Run 3B evaluation findings, Gemma 4 model tiers, security/governance, and glossary.
- `https://openclinai.org/wahs/walkthrough/` (`landing/wahs/walkthrough/index.html`): Mobile app walkthrough for general and clinical readers. Follows the synthetic patient Margaret through the WAHS mobile companion step by step (sign in, home, records, personal notes, Ask WAHS with citations, missing information, sharing). Screenshots are hosted on `gs://openclinai-media/wahs/mobile-walkthrough-20261126/`.
- `https://openclinai.org/wahs/sandbox/` (`landing/wahs/sandbox/index.html`): Research Sandbox guide for the public tool at `wahs-demo.openclinai.org`: the three tabs, connecting a hosted or local model, three guided exercises (large records, optional context, citations and missing information), and a glossary of sandbox terms.

Developer and local-stack setup lives in the "Running WAHS locally" block of `/wahs/architecture/#env-config`, not on the walkthrough pages.

Retired `/wahs/engine-reference/*`, `/wahs/engine-audit/*`, and `/med-agent-hub/wahs-architecture/*` paths redirect permanently (`308`) to `/wahs/architecture/` via `compose/website/Caddyfile`; `/wahs/design-report/*` redirects (`308`) to `/wahs/llm-strategy/`.
