Section 1 · Live Applications

Choose the right WAHS surface for your task

Every surface delegates 100% of FHIR extraction, condensation, enricher execution, and provenance mapping to wahs-engine.

Surface 1 · Public Inspection

WAHS Explorer Sandbox

URL: wahs-demo.openclinai.org ↗

Zero-business-logic thin UI connected to an isolated wahs-sandbox-engine (no HAPI/Keycloak access).

  • Step 1 (Public): Browse all 10 mounted real FHIR R4 bundles (Synthea, US Core, and MIMIC-IV-FHIR up to 22 MB) or upload any custom FHIR R4 JSON bundle.
  • Step 2 (Public): Call live POST /v1/wahs/context/preview to inspect exact Tier 0/1/2 condensation, chart_text, enricher suffix, system_prompt, and X-WAHS-Prefix-Hash.
  • Step 3 (UW GPU or Local BYO): Stream via your shared OpenClinAI Google session or forward the exact Step 2 engine prompt to LM Studio, Ollama, or llama-router with zero sign-in.

Section 2 · Sandbox Modes

Two ways to run Step 3 generation in the Explorer Sandbox

Steps 1 (FHIR resource browsing) and 2 (live wahs-sandbox-engine condensation & enricher preview) work immediately for every visitor with zero sign-in. For Step 3 live streaming, choose either option under Connection in the top bar:

Option A: Hosted UW CIRG GPU (Unified OpenClinAI Google Session)
  1. Open wahs-demo.openclinai.org ↗ and click Sign in for UW GPU (or sign in once at openclinai.org/wahs/gate/ ↗).
  2. The same HttpOnly session cookie authorizes wahealth.openclinai.org, wahs-web.openclinai.org, wahs-mobile.openclinai.org, and Step 3 streaming on wahs-demo.openclinai.org.
  3. Select wahs-26b (default), wahs-12b, or wahs-e4b and ask any clinical question.
Option B: Local BYO-Model (Zero Sign-In Required)
  1. Start any CORS-enabled local OpenAI-compatible server: llama-router (:8077), LM Studio (:1234), or Ollama (:11434).
  2. In the Sandbox top bar, click Connection, select Local BYO-Model, pick your local URL, and click Apply Connection.
  3. When you send a question, the Sandbox fetches the exact engine-assembled system_prompt and enrichment_text from Step 2 (/api/engine/v1/wahs/context/preview) and streams straight to your local model while preserving clickable [N] provenance drawers.

Section 3 · Reproducible Experiments

Three hands-on experiments to run in the Sandbox or Apps

Each experiment takes under two minutes and directly reproduces the behaviors measured in our November 26 H200 benchmark reports.

Experiment 1 · Budget & Tiers

Watch Tier 0 vs. Tier 2 on a 7,099-resource chart

See how wahs_core preserves the HL7 IPS safety core while logging exact omissions as budget shrinks.

  1. In Sandbox Step 1, select Maurice742 Auer97 (7,099 entries, 22 MB raw FHIR JSON).
  2. Open Step 2 and toggle the Context Budget across 64k, 16k, and 2k.
  3. Inspect Condensation Tier, Omitted Items, and Prefix Cache Hash in the metrics strip, then toggle back to 16k to observe an instant LRU prefix cache HIT.
What to observe: Under tight budgets (16k and 2k), Tier 2 folds repeated historical series and protects active allergies, meds, and conditions while recording exact omitted counts in [2] Completeness Manifest so note_rag and overflow_lookup can recover question-specific dropped facts.
Experiment 2 · Enrichers

Compare 0/5 (None) vs. 4/5 (Core) vs. 5/5 (All)

Reproduce Run 3A live by toggling individual modules in the multi-select Enrichers menu.

  1. Select Margaret Chen (geriatrics polypharmacy) or Elena Rostova (cardiorenal HFrEF + CKD3b).
  2. In Step 2, open the Enrichers checkbox menu and click Clear (0/5 None), then click Enricher Appendix.
  3. Click Core (4/5 Core) and then All (5/5 All) and compare the appended suffix tokens and disclosures.
What to observe: Notice that X-WAHS-Prefix-Hash stays completely unchanged when toggling enrichers because enrichers append strictly to Region 2 (turn suffix). With 4/5 Core, temporal and terminology_kb attach concise trend and drug-class facts; 5/5 All adds [K1] guideline snippets.
Experiment 3 · Grounding

Verify clickable [N] provenance & grounded abstention

Confirm every claim links to a real FHIR resource and that undocumented procedures trigger clean abstention.

  1. In Step 3 (or Web/Mobile Ask WAHS) on Margaret Chen, ask: "What active medications am I taking, and are there any fall or sedation risks?"
  2. Click any inline [N] pill in the streamed answer to inspect the exact FHIR R4 JSON resource and source clinic.
  3. Next, toggle Show suggested questions and ask: "Are there any records of a colonoscopy or cardiac stress test in my chart?"
What to observe: Medication claims cite only numeric [N] tags mapped to real FHIR UUIDs (zero fabricated pseudo-citation brackets). When asked about undocumented procedures, the model explicitly states no matching record exists rather than guessing.
