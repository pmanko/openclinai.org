Washington Health Summary (WAHS) · Pristine Baseline Audit

How the WAHS engine works and why its baseline is simple.

The WAHS Engine is a lean, standalone service that sits between the WA Health Summary app, the patient's health record store, and an open-weight language model. Following our November slim-down refactor, the default runtime is domain-neutral: pure-stdlib 3-tier verbatim-first condensation in wahs_core (1,776 LOC) + stateless OpenAI routing in wahs_engine (1,871 LOC), with experimental clinical enrichers cleanly isolated in wahs_extensions/ (unplugged by default) and patient-facing privacy/selective-disclosure policies deferred as unbuilt pending team design.

Updated 25 November 2026 · wahs-engine on GitHub ↗ (main at 3b9dcc0 via PR #13) · Suites: 13 unit/extension tests + 2 live HAPI/Keycloak integration checks + Docker smoke CI passing

Pristine baseline summary

  1. Domain-neutral core, unplugged extensions, and explicit privacy boundary. wahs_core (1,776 LOC pure Python stdlib verified under python -S) runs 3-tier verbatim-first condensation (Tier 0 verbatim ≤ 16,384 tokens; Tier 1 ontology-free RLE folding and Tier 2 packed only over budget). Optional enrichers (note_rag, overflow_lookup, temporal_trends, experimental.clinical_reconciliation) live unplugged in src/wahs_extensions/ behind wahs_core.ports.ContextEnricher, and premature regex privacy redaction (sharing_posture) was removed completely pending team design.
  2. Stateless runtime & clean wire IDs. wahs_engine (1,871 LOC) provides zero-privilege fhir-auth star-graph assembly, optional local RS256 JWKS verification, exact offline Gemma 4 / tiktoken budgeting, SSE <think> stripping, and clean model aliases (wahs-26b default, wahs-12b, wahs-e4b) aligned across shl-ips and wahs-mobile-frontend.
  3. Grounded in 1,800 H200 benchmark runs & hardened runtime invariants. November H200 evaluations showed condensing first to 16k beat 32k charts and 2k tool loops (Gemma 4 26B scored 85.1/100 with 0/75 unsafe); all hardened pooling, JWKS, SSE preflight, and FHIR R4 status invariants remain regression-tested on main (3b9dcc0).

High-level architecture

How a patient question moves through the system

Three core components do the work in a straight line: the patient app handles sign-in and displays citations, the WAHS Engine fetches scoped records and runs 3-tier verbatim-first condensation, and a stateless model server writes the cited reply. Optional clinical enrichers live in an unplugged shelf (wahs_extensions/) disabled by default.

Explicit scope & unbuilt privacy boundary: wahs-engine owns stateless per-turn FHIR bundle assembly via fhir-auth, 3-tier verbatim-first structural condensation, optional operator-configured wahs_extensions/ plugins, exact offline token budgeting, and x_wahs source-citation mapping. Patient privacy / selective-disclosure redaction policies are intentionally unbuilt in this baseline pending team design (premature regex heuristics and sharing_posture flags were removed so wahs_core never alters or hides clinical content implicitly). It also does not own OIDC login UI, persistent conversation databases, HAPI admin credentials, SMART Health Link cryptography (shl-server), GPU multi-model scheduling, or automated evaluation judging (validation-harness).

Research, standards & best-practice foundations

Why each step in the engine exists, and the sources behind it

Each of the four steps in the diagram above (plus our evaluation harness) implements established findings from long-context LLM research, healthcare interoperability standards, clinical practice guidelines, and privacy law.

Step 1 · Fetch & Check Freshness

Patient-mediated FHIR APIs and version-stamped caching

Patients gather records across clinics through Cures Act FHIR R4 APIs. Checking lightweight meta.lastUpdated version stamps before pulling full bundles keeps charts fresh in ~10 ms when nothing changed.

  • HL7 US Core (FHIR R4) ↗ & ONC Cures Act Final Rule ↗ · Standard patient-access clinical profiles and Patient/$everything retrieval.
  • SMART Health Links ↗ & HL7 FHIR DS4P Security Labels ↗ · Patient-mediated sharing and meta.security access scoping via uwcirg/jwt-proxy ↗.
  • HL7 FHIR RESTful Versioning (meta.lastUpdated) ↗ · Optimistic cache revalidation across multi-clinic datasets (session & scope design →).
Step 2 · Condense Record (90–99%)

Long-context attention limits and clinical safety priority

Language models lose recall when key facts sit buried in noisy multi-megabyte JSON. Pinning an international clinical safety core first and explicitly logging omitted counts prevents silent truncation and guessing.

  • Liu et al., Lost in the Middle (TACL, 2024) ↗ · Demonstrates U-shaped long-context recall degradation, motivating compact 16k condensation over raw 200k+ FHIR dumps.
  • HL7 International Patient Summary (IPS) & ISO 27269 ↗ · Defines the mandatory safety core (allergies, active meds, active problems, implants) preserved first across all budgets.
  • Robertson & Zaragoza, Okapi BM25 (2009) ↗ · Grounds exact clinical-term passage retrieval (note_rag) when long hospital notes exceed budget.
Optional Shelf · wahs_extensions/ (Unplugged)

Isolated R&D enrichers & unbuilt privacy boundary

Domain-specific reconciliation and retrieval helpers sit cleanly behind the ContextEnricher port (unplugged unless enabled by the operator), while patient-facing selective-disclosure rules wait for dedicated team privacy design rather than living inside core regexes.

  • HL7 Clinical Information Reconciliation (CIRI) ↗ & WHO SMART Guidelines ↗ · Experimental graph/guideline rules preserved under wahs_extensions/experimental/ (Med Agent Hub →).
  • 42 CFR Part 2 ↗ & WA My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373) ↗ · Regulatory requirements preserved for future W9 DS4P / SD-JWT design; zero premature regexes live in wahs_core.
Step 3 · Stateless Prompt & [N] Links

Prefix KV-cache reuse, inline citations, and human review

Keeping messages[0] identical across questions lets GPU servers reuse cached attention blocks (cutting follow-ups from ~19s to ~3s), while [1]..[N] source links make every claim inspectable.

  • Kwon et al., PagedAttention / vLLM (SOSP, 2023) ↗ & Zheng et al., SGLang / RadixAttention (NeurIPS, 2024) ↗ · Prefix KV-cache reuse motivating the invariant messages[0] / turn-local messages[-1] split.
  • Gao et al., ALCE: Generating Text with Citations (EMNLP, 2023) ↗ & PhysioNet ArchEHR-QA (BioNLP 2025) ↗ · Inline bracketed [N] evidence grounding over EHR records.
  • HL7 AI Transparency on FHIR (AI-Provenance) ↗ & Amershi et al. (CHI, 2019) / Microsoft HAX ↗ · Progressive source disclosure and explicit human-review signaling.
Cross-Cutting · Blinded Evaluation & Claim Entailment

Why we verify outside the model with physician rubrics, atomic entailment, and two-judge consensus

Recent clinical AI evaluations show that LLM self-verification accepts incorrect medical answers over 60% of the time (Jin et al., 2026 ↗). Instead of asking the answering model to grade itself, the OpenClinAI Validation Harness → combines deterministic budget/citation checks with atomic EHR fact verification and two independent blinded judges:

  • HealthBench (OpenAI, 2025) ↗ & Stanford MedHELM ↗ · Multi-axis rubric scoring with strict-majority clinical harm consensus across published WAHS benchmark packets ↗.
  • Chung et al., VeriFact (2025) ↗ & Tang et al., MiniCheck (EMNLP, 2024) ↗ · Atomic claim-to-EHR entailment checking so cited [N] records genuinely support stated values, dates, and statuses.
  • Angelopoulos et al., Prediction-Powered Inference (Science, 2023) ↗ · Statistical framework for calibrating automated judge scores against clinician adjudication, paired with interactive inspection in the WAHS Explorer Sandbox ↗.

Functional audit

Verified subsystem guarantees across wahs_core and wahs_engine

Inside the slimmed repository, the pure-stdlib core library (src/wahs_core/, 1,776 LOC) handles verbatim-first structural extraction and condensation with zero domain heuristics, while the thin FastAPI runtime (src/wahs_engine/, 1,871 LOC) handles authentication, byte-bounded caching, exact token counting, and SSE streaming. Research enrichers live unplugged in src/wahs_extensions/ (1,152 LOC Python + 4 sample rule packs), and persona seeding lives in src/wahs_devtools/.

1. Record intake, 3-tier condensation, and unplugged extensions (wahs_core & wahs_extensions)

Verified on main (3b9dcc0)

3-tier verbatim-first condensation

  • Verbatim by default (Tier 0): When the complete extracted record and full decoded clinical notes fit inside budget (default 16,384 tokens), emits every item verbatim (folded_items_count = 0).
  • Ontology-free RLE & packing (Tier 1 / 2): Only over budget, folds identical repeated labs/renewals strictly by FHIR code/unit and binary-searches a monotonic recency prefix around the HL7 IPS safety core.
  • No note splicing: Long notes over budget use clean 400-char head previews without heuristic medication-hold regex splicing.
Unplugged by default

wahs_extensions/ & ContextEnricher

  • Strict isolation: WAHS_ENABLED_ENRICHERS="" by default ensures wahs_extensions is never even imported into sys.modules in standard baseline deployments.
  • Opt-in enrichers: Operators can explicitly enable note_rag (single-patient Okapi BM25), overflow_lookup (Tier-0 note recovery), temporal_trends (slope math), or experimental.clinical_reconciliation, attaching provenance under x_wahs.enrichers.
Clean R4 & Privacy Boundary

FHIR R4 statuses & unbuilt privacy

  • Invalidated status filtering: Excludes entered-in-error and not-done entries across FHIR R4 clinical resources, demotes refuted allergies, and deduplicates notes via SHA-256 while preserving multi-coded RxNorm/SNOMED CT/LOINC/CVX labels.
  • Privacy explicitly unbuilt: All premature regex redaction rules (sharing_posture, classify_sensitivity*) were removed; patient selective disclosure remains a separate, future design milestone.

2. Per-patient caching, token sizing, and live streaming (wahs_engine)

Verified on main (3b9dcc0)

Live freshness, JWKS defense-in-depth & exact tokenizers

  • Checks fhir-auth on every turn: Verifies MasterPatient ownership and linked clinic dataset meta.lastUpdated stamps before reading byte-bounded LRU caches, blocks internal HAPI bypass URLs via parsed hostname checks, and supports optional local pure-stdlib RS256 JWKS verification.
  • Offline token accuracy: Bundled Gemma 4 and tiktoken tokenizers match GPU prompt token counts within <0.01% completely offline, trimming oldest chat turns cleanly and forwarding caller prompts verbatim when extensions are off.
Single-Turn Clean Runtime

Streaming preflight, worker threads & SDK compatibility

  • Preflight status guarantees: Validates prompt budgets and opens the upstream LLM stream before committing HTTP 200 SSE headers so invalid turns or upstream outages return clean HTTP 400, 422, or 502 responses, stripping unclosed <think> blocks on truncation.
  • Vanilla OpenAI mode: Streams tokens cleanly in a single southbound pass (zero multi-round LLM tool loops or reset_content rewinds), supports wahs.emit_stream_events: false, and offloads CPU condensation via asyncio.to_thread.

Code audit & slim baseline ledger

Hardened runtime invariants & November slim baseline refactor (3b9dcc0)

Earlier audits surfaced 27 runtime and clinical findings across wahs_engine (E1–E11) and wahs_core (C1–C10). After merging those fixes at 808d16a, PR #13 (3b9dcc0) slimmed the default engine from 17,325 LOC to a 3,647-LOC domain-neutral core + runtime, moved research enrichers into the unplugged wahs_extensions/ catalog, and deleted premature privacy regexes and Synthea/MIMIC hacks while retaining all hardened pooling, JWKS, SSE preflight, and FHIR R4 status invariants.

  1. Slimmed core baseline & isolated extension shelf on wahs-engine#13 Merged · PR #13 (3b9dcc0)

    Distilled src/wahs_core/ to 1,776 LOC pure stdlib (verified via python3 -S), slimmed src/wahs_engine/ to 1,871 LOC with clean model IDs (wahs-26b, wahs-12b, wahs-e4b) and clean WahsRequestOptions, isolated note_rag / overflow_lookup / temporal_trends / experimental.clinical_reconciliation behind wahs_core.ports.ContextEnricher (WAHS_ENABLED_ENRICHERS="" default), moved persona seeding to src/wahs_devtools/seeder.py (wahs-seed-hapi), and removed premature sharing_posture privacy heuristics and 2k LLM tool loops.

  2. Merged 12-persona stack & runtime pooling fixes on wahs-engine#10 Verified · PR #10

    Shared an app-level httpx.AsyncClient pool so single-flight dataset coalescing never closes a disconnected request's socket under a concurrent caller (E1), capped @<budget> suffixes at 131,072 tokens / model context windows (E2), wrote refreshed datasets back into _dataset_cache on refresh=True (E3), and resolved cross-key model aliases and Qwen tokenizers (E10).

  3. Hardened wahs_core clinical, FHIR R4 status, and privacy invariants Verified · PR #12

    Scanned reasons, referenced conditions, notes, and clinic names at extraction and tool-lookup time (C1), enforced fail-closed validate_sharing_posture() with expanded MOUD/ARV/WA MHMDA lexical terms (C2), filtered entered-in-error and not-done statuses across allergies, procedures, child panel labs, notes, and appointments (C3), extracted DiagnosticReport.conclusion and list-valued dosages (C4), preserved electrolyte salts (C5), hardened word-boundary note cues and directional low-HbA1c rules (C6), separated trajectory units and on-hold meds (C7–C9), and locked concurrent FTS5 queries (C10).

  4. Hardened streaming preflight, JWKS defense-in-depth, and worker offload Verified · PR #12

    Added zero-dependency RFC 8017 RS256 JWKS verification, urlsplit internal-HAPI hostname blocking, and explicit MasterPatient ownership checks (E4), stripped unclosed <think> blocks on length cutoffs (E5), validated prompt budgets and Round-0 upstream streams before committing HTTP 200 SSE headers (E6), added wahs.emit_stream_events: false for vanilla OpenAI SDKs and guarded malformed tool JSON (E7–E8), and offloaded CPU condensation/BM25 via asyncio.to_thread with byte-bounded LRU caches and full OpenAI parameter forwarding (E9, E11).

  5. Synchronized connected frontends, harness, and reference docs Verified · All Merged

    Merged shl-ips#2/#3, wahs-mobile-frontend#2/#3, validation-harness#219, and openclinai.org documentation so repository docs, API contracts, and public architecture pages reflect the exact verified baseline ahead of UW CIRG team strategy sync and EHIgnite Phase 2 partner stress testing.
