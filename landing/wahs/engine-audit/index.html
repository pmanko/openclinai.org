Washington Health Summary (WAHS) · Architectural & Functional Audit

WAHS engine architectural and functional audit.

A code-level architectural and functional report on wahs-engine as it operates today (19 November 2026): how the standalone middleware connects patient-mediated WAHS frontends, zero-privilege HAPI FHIR proxies, and open-weight GPU inference; what is verified on main (85fb799) versus open full-stack pull requests (PR #10 at 193cf1a); and the concrete clinical, privacy, concurrency, and contract findings surfaced by a full module-by-module audit.

Audit date: 19 November 2026 · Audited revisions: wahs-engine@85fb799 (main) ↗ & PR #10 (193cf1a) ↗ · Test verification: 81 passed on main, 86 passed on PR #10

Executive assessment

  1. Clean decoupled foundation on main (85fb799): wahs-engine cleanly separates a pure-Python stdlib clinical library (wahs_core, 10 modules + 5 static clinical KB packs) from a 6-dependency FastAPI OpenAI /v1 service (wahs_engine, 8 modules) running non-root (65532:65532) with offline Gemma 4 and BPE tokenizers.
  2. Strong empirical clinical & safety performance: Across two 900-cell blinded two-judge H200 benchmarks, Gemma 4 26B + 4-Layer KB at a 16k chart budget achieved 85.1/100 (84.1 safety-adjusted, 0/75 confirmed unsafe answers, 3.42s median p50 latency, 100% budget compliance, 99.78% inline [N] citation UUID resolution, and 0/900 third-party privacy leaks).
  3. Active cross-repo frontier & targeted remediation queue: Full-stack 12-persona demos (shl-ips#2, wahs-mobile-frontend#2, and local 10-service Compose) currently target open wahs-engine#10. Before merging PR #10, three runtime regressions introduced on that branch (request-local HTTP client closure under single-flight coalescing, unbounded @<budget> GPU num_ctx allocation, and refresh=True dataset cache writeback) plus high-priority wahs_core status and sensitivity edge cases should be resolved.

High-level system architecture

End-to-end dataflow, trust boundaries, and 4-layer clinical pipeline

The diagram below maps how wahs-engine sits between patient-controlled client BFFs, the zero-privilege fhir-auth HAPI FHIR R4 boundary, and stateless OpenAI-compatible model hosts. Every chat turn splits its prompt into a query-invariant chart prefix (messages[0], cached in GPU VRAM across follow-up turns) and a turn-local enrichment suffix (messages[-1], carrying precomputed temporal horizons, clinical entity graph edges, matched guidelines, and optional note passages).

Foundational aspects & standards links

What we are building on: clinical standards, privacy law, and shared OpenClinAI primitives

Every design choice inside wahs-engine traces back to four foundational commitments: standards-based patient-mediated health data exchange, non-destructive deterministic context engineering, zero-privilege authorization with selective disclosure, and blinded empirical safety verification. Use the links below to inspect each specification, repository, and benchmark artifact directly.

Pillar 1 · Interoperability & Provenance

Patient-mediated FHIR R4 summaries and verifiable AI transparency

Rather than inventing a proprietary health record schema, WAHS preserves standard FHIR R4 resources end-to-end, prioritizes the International Patient Summary safety core under tight token budgets, and separates source clinical evidence from AI synthesis.

  • HL7 International Patient Summary (IPS) & ISO 27269 ↗ · Defines the mandatory safety core (allergies, active medications, active problems, active goals, and implants) preserved first across all condensation tiers.
  • HL7 US Core Implementation Guide (FHIR R4) ↗ & SMART Health Links ↗ · Ground longitudinal multi-clinic imports and encrypted A256GCM JWE summary sharing.
  • HL7 AI Transparency on FHIR (AI-Provenance) ↗ · Emitted in x_wahs.ai_provenance alongside human_review_required: true and inline [N] UUID mappings.
  • WAHS Project Overview → & UW CIRG & WA DOH Reference Application ↗ · Boundary between the patient-facing PHR pilot and the OpenClinAI R&D workbench.
Pillar 2 · Context & Clinical Rules

Deterministic 2-stage condensation and pre-generation clinical graphs

Open-weight 4B–26B models fail silently when multi-megabyte raw FHIR JSON overflows context windows or buries cross-clinic medication holds inside thousands of routine entries. Deterministic Python code normalizes, condenses, and links clinical entities before prompt assembly.

  • WAHS AI Strategy & Model Selection → & WAHS Engine Reference → · Architectural rationale, KV-cache mechanics, and wire examples.
  • pmanko/wahs-engine Repository ↗ & Med Agent Hub Shared Engine → · Standalone pure-stdlib wahs_core consumed upstream by Med Agent Hub.
  • KDIGO 2024 CKD Guidelines ↗ & ADA Diabetes Standards ↗ · Ground SNOMED stage-progression rules, eGFR dosing cutoffs, and potassium hazard edges.
  • NLM RxNorm ↗, LOINC ↗ & CDC ACIP Schedules ↗ · Normalize active ingredients, cross-reactivity classes, lab trajectories, and CVX intervals.
Pillar 3 · Zero-Privilege Auth & Privacy

Per-user security labels and selective external-share redaction

wahs-engine holds zero administrative credentials to HAPI FHIR and stores zero PHI transcripts on the model server. Privacy postures separate full patient self-review from sensitive-record exclusion during external sharing.

  • uwcirg/jwt-proxy (fhir-auth) ↗ · Verifies Keycloak RS256 tokens and enforces meta.security.code == jwt.sub on every HAPI query.
  • 42 CFR Part 2 ↗, WA My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373) ↗ & HL7 FHIR DS4P ↗ · Inform external_share redaction of substance-use, behavioral-health, and sensitive social-history records.
  • pmanko/shl-ips Web BFF ↗ & pmanko/wahs-mobile-frontend Mobile BFF ↗ · Partition browser chat state by (patientId, sharingPosture, chartScope) so switching to external_share never forwards prior sensitive turns.

Subsystem audit 1 · src/wahs_core/

Functional audit of the 4-layer clinical core

wahs_core transforms raw FHIR R4 bundles into numbered, token-budgeted clinical ledgers and structured pre-generation clinical context without mutating source records or calling external networks.

Layers 0 & 1: Normalization, provenance attribution, and FHIR R4 resource coverage

extract_bundle() (extractors.py ↗) runs a two-pass index over bundle entries, resolves source clinic organizations through a five-step fallback chain (direct serviceProvider/recorder/asserter/requester → performer[] → Encounter.serviceProvider → inverted Provenance.target[] → wahealthsummary-source-name meta tag), deduplicates base64 clinical notes by SHA-256 across DocumentReference and DiagnosticReport.presentedForm, and emits typed ClinicalItem (across 23 clinical sections) and NumericPoint records.

FHIR R4 Resource Category Extracted Types / Sections Current Behavior & Code-Audit Coverage Notes
IPS Mandatory Safety Core Patient, AllergyIntolerance, active Condition, active Medication*, active Goal, implant Device Pinned with mandatory=True across all budgets. Audit finding: only active Condition filters refuted/entered-in-error; erroneous or refuted AllergyIntolerance records currently retain mandatory=True, and MedicationStatement.dosage (a FHIR JSON list rather than dict) is silently skipped at extractors.py:1022.
Labs, Vitals, Notes & Imaging Observation, DiagnosticReport, DocumentReference, ImagingStudy Extracts numeric LOINC points (suppressing entered-in-error/cancelled points), comparator strings (<, >=), and base64 UTF-8 notes. Audit finding: DiagnosticReport.conclusion is never read, reports lacking both result[] and inline base64 presentedForm.data are dropped, and DocumentReference ignores docStatus="entered-in-error" and URL-only attachments.
Procedures, Immunizations, CarePlans & Visits Procedure, Immunization, CarePlan, Encounter, Appointment Separates high-acuity hospitalizations from routine visits, classifies terminal vs. planned vs. in-progress dates, and evaluates ACIP vaccine intervals. Audit finding: Procedure omits status filtering and display (rendering not-done like completed procedures), and cancelled/noshow Appointments receive date_kind="planned".
Counted Admin vs. Unhandled US Core Types 9 billing types + 6 lookup types counted in excluded_resource_counts Explicitly strips and counts Claim, ExplanationOfBenefit, Coverage, etc. Coverage gap: US Core / IPS types ServiceRequest, QuestionnaireResponse, FamilyMemberHistory, CareTeam, Composition, Consent, Flag, and AdverseEvent are neither extracted nor tallied in excluded_resource_counts.

Layers 2 & 3: Monotonic 2-stage condensation, 7-edge entity graph, and temporal digest

Layer 2 · condenser.py

Monotonic Stage-A & Stage-B condensation

  • Stage A fast-path: Applies RLE folding to historical meds, resolved conditions, routine visits, and >3-dose routine vaccine series; builds LOINC trajectories (initial, latest, min, max, n, trend); groups intraday vitals; and prepends [1] Patient + [2] Completeness Manifest.
  • Stage B budget packing: Excerpts notes >400 chars while splicing tail medication-hold directives via _extract_note_conflict_clauses, enforces primary section quotas (pass_tier=0) to prevent lab floods from starving clinical notes, and binary-searches the exact largest optional prefix under token_counter.
  • 512-token floor: Falls back smoothly through compressed_mandatory to build_ultra micro-capsules while preserving every constituent FHIR UUID in memberUuids.
Layer 3a · clinical_graph.py

11 node categories & 7 typed clinical edges

  • High-priority safety edges (always eligible): CROSS_REACTS_WITH (RxNorm allergy cross-reactivity), CONTRAINDICATED_OR_DOSE_ADJUST_IN (UCUM-normalized eGFR, creatinine, directional potassium, INR, hemoglobin, glucose), CONFLICTS_WITH (duplicate therapy & stage conflicts), and CONTRADICTED_OR_MODIFIED_BY_NOTE (chronological hold-vs-resume reconciliation).
  • Query-gated & provenance edges: MONITORED_BY and TREATS fire only when matched to query concepts and cap cleanly when high-priority alerts are dense; REPORTED_BY_ORG tracks multi-clinic provenance.
  • Tier-0 overflow & FTS5 KB: Recovers omitted items on tight budgets with sequential [N+1..M] citations and queries in-memory SQLite FTS5 over wahs_corpus.jsonl.
Layer 3b · temporal_digest.py

Deterministic temporal horizons & privacy

  • Realized reference anchor: Computes reference_date strictly from completed, non-invalidated, non-administrative, non-redacted clinical events so future care-plan end dates or device manufacture dates never skew relative time windows.
  • Tie-safe lab trends: Refuses UUID tie-breaking on ambiguous same-day boundary values, suppresses single-point trends (n=1), and emits 6-month/12-month horizons plus upcoming vs. fulfilled appointments.
  • external_share isolation: Filters SDOH/PRIVACY-SENSITIVE items prior to Stage-A condensation, graph construction, KB code extraction, temporal digest calculation, and BM25 note chunking.

Subsystem audit 2 · src/wahs_engine/

Runtime service, caching, offline token sizing, and SSE orchestration

wahs_engine wraps wahs_core in a stateless FastAPI middleware exposing GET /healthz, GET /v1/models, POST /v1/wahs/context/preview, and POST /v1/chat/completions. Note that callers must use dedicated /v1/wahs/context/preview for context warm-up; WahsRequestOptions sets extra="forbid" and rejects unknown fields with HTTP 422.

Runtime Mechanism Verified Implementation (main @ 85fb799) Operational Caveats & PR #10 Changes
JWT Binding & fhir-auth Enforcement
(jwt_claims.py, assembler.py)		 Extracts jwt.sub and enforces live HTTP probes against fhir_auth_base_url (MasterPatient + all linked Patient/{ds_id} stubs) before consulting in-memory caches. Rewrites HAPI _getpages links back onto fhir-auth and blocks external_share on truncated fetches with HTTP 409. decode_and_bind_jwt() does not verify RS256 signatures locally; security depends on fhir_auth_base_url pointing to uwcirg/jwt-proxy (currently guarded only by checking "fhir-internal" not in url). Also, HAPI does not bump Patient.meta.lastUpdated on in-place child resource edits without wahs.refresh=True.
In-Memory LRU Caches & Multi-Worker State
(assembler.py)		 _dataset_cache (256 entries, 1800s TTL) keyed on (sub, ds_id, lastUpdated) and _prefix_cache (128 entries, 1800s TTL) keyed on (sub, datasets_fp, scope_hash). Clones citation_map per turn to prevent cross-request bleed. Caches are per-process and unbounded by byte size (large decoded clinical notes across multiple budgets can consume several GBs). Running multi-worker uvicorn --workers N splits cache hits and refresh=True invalidations across workers.
Offline Tokenizers & Window Reserves
(tokenizers_registry.py, assembler.py)		 Bundles gemma4_tokenizer.json.gz and pre-cached tiktoken (o200k_base/cl100k_base) for zero-network exact sizing (<0.01% delta vs. Ollama). Deducts system framing, 12.5% enrichment/output reserves, and 15% conversation reserve, clamping to a 16,384-token default chart ceiling; _trim_history_to_fit pops oldest turns first. PR #10 adds @<budget> model suffixes (e.g. wahs-26b-kb@32k) that auto-lift chart_budget_ceiling and context_window, but currently lacks an upper cap on @<budget> (see Finding E2).
SSE Streaming, Tool Loop & <think> Stripping
(upstream.py)		 Supports litellm/ollama (num_ctx, reasoning_effort="none"), llamacpp (enable_thinking=False), and openai flavors. Caps parallel tool calls at 4 per round (pairing skipped calls with error tool messages) and flags suspected_upstream_truncation. On main, stream=True buffers Round 1 whenever overflow tools are attached. PR #10 adds 96-char live lookahead streaming + x_wahs_stream events (context_ready, tool_round/reset_content), though reset_content requires WAHS-aware SSE parsers.
