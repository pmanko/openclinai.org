OpenClinAI / Projects / WA Health Summary (WAHS)

UW CIRG & WA Department of Health Collaboration

Patient-mediated health summaries from real-world FHIR R4 records.

People receiving care across multiple clinics, hospitals, and pharmacies accumulate fragmented multi-megabyte FHIR R4 exports filled with repeated lab panels, duplicate notes, and conflicting medication orders. wahs-engine is our standalone, zero-privilege middleware that pools a signed-in patient's records via fhir-auth, condenses them verbatim-first, layers five modular clinical enrichers, and streams source-cited answers from open-weight models pinned at 64k context.

End-to-End Pipeline

Five stages from fragmented FHIR exports to verifiable answers

01 · FHIR Sources & Zero-Privilege Auth

Forward caller JWTs through fhir-auth

wahs-engine holds zero HAPI admin credentials. It forwards the patient's Keycloak token to fhir-auth, pools linked clinic datasets plus patient-authored My Story entries, and checks meta.lastUpdated stamps in ~10 ms.

02 · Verbatim-First Condensation

Keep charts verbatim when they fit; fold smoothly when tight

wahs_core strips FHIR JSON overhead and duplicate notes via SHA-256, keeping records verbatim at Tier 0 whenever capacity allows, and otherwise folding repeated series (Tier 1) and protecting the HL7 IPS safety core ↗ (Tier 2) with explicit omission accounting.

03 · Modular Enricher Suffix

Five generalizable enrichers with prefix KV-cache stability

core-enriched activates four generalizable modules (temporal, terminology_kb, note_rag, overflow_lookup, plus optional guideline_kb) strictly in the turn suffix so the leading chart prefix stays warm in GPU VRAM.

04 · 64k Streaming & Provenance

Verify every claim against its FHIR R4 source

OpenAI /v1 streaming runs against wahs-26b, 12b, or e4b pinned to a warm 64k num_ctx, emitting immediate context_ready metadata, 15s keepalives, clickable [N] source drawers, and HL7 AI-Provenance.

Published November 26, 2026 Evaluations

Blinded two-judge H200 readouts backing our shipped defaults

Every configuration default in wahs-engine (wahs-26b, 16k chart budget, core-enriched 4/5 enrichers, and 64k warm num_ctx) is grounded in blinded two-judge evaluations published on reports.openclinai.org.

Run 3A · n = 225 Cells
+7.5 to +7.6 pts

32k Modular Enrichers Ablation: core-enriched (4/5 modules) lifted Gemma 4 26B from 72.3 to 79.8 / 100 (0 confirmed unsafe) and Gemma 4 12B from 76.5 to 84.1 / 100 (0 confirmed unsafe).

Run 3B · n = 144 Cells
85.2 / 100 @ 16k

16k Tier-2 Retrieval Recovery: On dense longitudinal charts with excerpted/omitted notes, rag-overflow lifted Gemma 4 26B at 16k by +8.0 pts (77.2 → 85.2, 0 unsafe), beating un-enriched 32k (76.3) at half the token budget.

Budget Sweep · n = 192 Cells
+8.1 to +10.6 pts

16k to 128k Budget Sweep: Condensing multi-year records into 16k–32k outperformed unbounded 128k stuffing across every Gemma 4 tier (26B: 74.1 → 82.2; 12B: 71.1 → 81.7) while reducing confirmed unsafe answers.

Health Data & AI Transparency Standards

  • HL7 International Patient Summary (IPS) & ISO 27269↗ Defines the mandatory safety core (allergies, active medications, active problems, implants) preserved first under Tier 2 budgets.
  • HL7 US Core (FHIR R4) & SMART Health Links↗ Longitudinal record retrieval and patient-mediated sharing specifications implemented by WA Health Summary.
  • HL7 AI Transparency on FHIR (AI-Provenance)↗ Emitted on every response to separate supporting FHIR records, recorded model identity, active enrichers, and human review requirements.
  • CIEL, NLM RxNorm, LOINC & CDC ACIP↗ Open public standards powering the deterministic terminology_kb enricher without vendor-proprietary black boxes.
