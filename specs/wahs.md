# WA Health Summary (WAHS) condensation, reconciliation and privacy

Patient-mediated clinical summarization, cross-clinic FHIR R4 reconciliation, privacy segmentation, and citation-grounded Q&A developed in collaboration with UW CIRG and the Washington State Department of Health.

## Foundational specs and background context

- **Umbrella architecture & evaluation protocols:**
  - [Architecture boundaries](architecture.md) — separation between umbrella publication, `med-agent-hub` runtime/condensation, and `validation-harness` offline/HTTP evaluation.
  - [Checked answers (`specs/checked-answers.md`)](checked-answers.md) — 10-stage Hub verification pipeline, cross-family label-only review (`single-12b-xcheck`), and narrow citation entailment grounder.
  - [Confidence labels (`specs/confidence-labels.md`)](confidence-labels.md) — check precision, avoiding false contradiction flags, and disabling `dry_multiplier` repetition penalty corruption on clinical lists.
  - [Blind multi-judge protocol (`specs/judging.md`)](judging.md) — blinded multi-pass judging, judge cards, and strip-labels evaluation.
  - [Provider parity & local inference efficiency (`specs/provider-parity.md`)](provider-parity.md) — reading chart context once per request and reusing temporal facts on the local Mac `llama-router`.

## Separation of evaluation modalities

Every WAHS evaluation run declares its `evaluation_modality` in `run_manifest.json` and `meta.json`, staged via [`scripts/stage-report.py`](../scripts/stage-report.py) and rendered with a distinct modality badge in [`scripts/build-reports-index.py`](../scripts/build-reports-index.py):

1. **`deterministic_pipeline` (`Deterministic Pipeline Gate`):** Evaluates the deterministic Python 5-Tier Condenser and 4-Layer Clinical KB rules (token budget compliance, IPS safety core retention, deterministic conflict recall, and zero-leakage privacy redaction). Reported as deterministic gate pass rates, never conflated with generative LLM judge scores.
2. **`condensation_retrieval_ablation` (`Condensation & Retrieval Ablation`):** Evaluates information retention across `Tier 1 (16k)` through `Tier 4 (2k)` static condensation vs. `Tier 4 (2k) + On-Demand Full-Chart Lookup Tools` stratified by question horizon (*Current Active State*, *24-Month Trend*, *Deep Historical >2 Years*, *Full-Text Note Detail*).
3. **`live_model_and_team_eval` (`Live Model & KB Team Evaluation`):** Evaluates live local open-weight models and multi-agent KB setups over identical condensed inputs, scored on the standard OpenClinAI `scout` axes (`Benchmark /100`, `Accuracy`, `Completeness`, `Relevance`, `Unsafe answers`) with per-question `comparison.html` and `dashboard.html` drill-down.

- [ ] **W12 · Open Architectural Question — Non-Destructive Structural Condensation vs. Skill-Based Chart Hygiene & Deduplication ([Design Report §9.8](../landing/wahs/design-report/index.html#non-destructive-condensation)):** Decouple **content-preserving syntactic serialization** from **semantic deduplication, clinical conflict tagging, and sensitive-record redaction** (`extractors.py` / `condenser.py`):
  - **Audit of current coupling risks:** Currently, `extract_bundle()` and `condense_bundle_to_budget()` interleave four distinct operations: (1) lossless syntactic stripping of FHIR JSON boilerplate, (2) heuristic semantic folding (stripping `# Medications` / `# HPI` markdown headers in `_extract_unique_note_narrative()`, deduplicating numeric lab/vital observations by calendar date `YYYY-MM-DD` in `_build_lab_trajectories()` and `_group_vitals_by_date()`, and folding stopped medications by ingredient in `_fold_stopped_medications()`), (3) pre-annotating `[conflict_note]` directly into extracted items so the condenser knows what not to fold, and (4) unconditionally running `classify_sensitivity()` (`[sensitivity: SDOH/PRIVACY-SENSITIVE]`) and `sharing_posture` redaction inside the base extractor/condenser rather than keeping privacy redaction optional in the deferred Secure FHIR Bundle workstream (`W9`).
  - **Target 2-Stage Architecture (`condense_mode="lossless_structural"` + Downstream Chart-Hygiene Skills + Optional `W9` Secure Bundle Layer):**
    1. **Stage 1 — Content-Invariant Structural Serialization & Windowing:** By default, strip only FHIR JSON transport overhead (`coding[]` system URIs, `urn:uuid:` prefixes, non-clinical `Claim`/`ExplanationOfBenefit` resources) and deduplicate *only* cryptographic byte-identical attachments (`SHA-256` hash match across `DocumentReference` and `DiagnosticReport.presentedForm` while preserving all `memberUuids`), with **no automatic conflict mutation, no semantic merging, and no built-in sensitivity tagging/redaction** (moving optional sensitivity redaction into `W9`). Keep all active problems, active medications, allergies, immunizations, and recent labs/notes **100% verbatim**, and expose older historical entries verbatim via the `W7` overflow lookup tools (`search_chart_entries`, `get_lab_history`, `read_clinical_note`) plus an explicit `[Completeness Manifest]`.
    2. **Stage 2 — Deduplication & Reconciliation as Explicit Agentic Skills Surfaced as Findings:** Move duplicate detection (e.g., same-day vaccines logged by both clinic and state registry, duplicate active prescriptions across clinics, repeated problem-list entries) and clinical conflict detection out of the ingestion extractor and into explicit, auditable **Chart-Hygiene & Reconciliation Skills** that run over the unmutated chart and surface structured **Findings** (`DuplicateCandidate` / `ReconciliationFinding` cards with `Source A vs. Source B` provenance) for patient/clinician review rather than silently cleaning or altering the chart representation.
