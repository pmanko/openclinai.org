04 · Use Case B-2: Privacy-Segmented Sharing (patient_self vs. external_share)

Under Layer 4 of the WAHS Clinical Knowledge Base (42 CFR Part 2, WA My Health My Data Act, and sensitive SDOH/IPV taxonomy rules), switching from patient_self to external_share applies a deterministic pre-condensation redaction gate:

  • patient_self Posture: Retains sensitive SDOH entries (such as SNOMED 706893006 Victim of intimate partner abuse) with an explicit patient-visible privacy badge so the patient can review their complete record.
  • external_share Posture: Strips all sensitive SDOH / IPV / substance-use / housing-instability resources prior to Tier 1–4 formatting and Q&A generation, achieving 0.0% sensitive UUID or string leakage across all token budgets.
